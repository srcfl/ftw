A charger with no car, which FTW asks for nothing, no longer says FTW lost control when it stops reporting. An idle Easee can go quiet once the car leaves, and the app then showed "Charger · Lost control" while the car was out on the road. Such a charger now says it waits for a car; one that goes quiet with a car on it, or while FTW asks it to charge, still raises the alarm.
