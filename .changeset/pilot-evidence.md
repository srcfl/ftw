Expose fresh effective EV pilot current separately from the dynamic current setting. A confirmed lower pilot that explains measured delivery is an informational charger limitation even when its internal cause is unknown. Stale or future pilot evidence cannot explain an EV shortfall. A pilot that is only past its 90 s window reads as unknown, and the driver's reported limit, such as Easee's load balancing, still stands.
