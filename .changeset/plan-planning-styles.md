The Plan card's forecast slider is now five planning styles, from Very careful to Very bold. A line under them says how much of the spare sun the plan counts on, and another says whether extra sun goes into the battery or to the grid. The default style, Balanced, holds back a smaller forecast margin than before (k 0.3 instead of 1), and a box that still runs the old default moves to it once. Settings → Planner fine-tunes the margin and saves at once.
