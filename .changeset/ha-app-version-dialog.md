In the Home Assistant app, the version in the header and the Version button under More now open a dialog. It names the running version and says Home Assistant installs updates and rollbacks for the app. Before, a click did nothing. On a Docker install, the dialog says FTW does not update itself there and to update it the way it was installed. On both, the header also shows the connection light and the optimizer warning again; they were hidden along with the update check.
