The car's fuse cut now acts on what the site meter shows. A cut the meter already shows no longer counts as still on its way, so when the house adds load after it, FTW cuts again once the fuse's heat budget is spent instead of waiting up to three minutes. A raise that is on its way but no longer fits now comes off before it lands, when it would take a phase above 1.2 times the fuse rating, or over the line on a phase whose heat budget is spent.
