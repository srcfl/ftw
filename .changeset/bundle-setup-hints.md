The bundled drivers carry a setup hint: one sentence on what to switch on in the device before FTW can reach it, such as Modbus TCP on the inverter. Fifteen bundled drivers have one (ctek, ctek_hybrid, ctek_v2, esphome_dsmr, fronius, goodwe, huawei, kostal, nibe_local, sma, sma_pv, solis, sungrow, victron, zap). Only the version and the hint changed in each.
