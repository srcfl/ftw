The FTW app can be turned on from Settings instead of by hand-editing YAML. The app tab now carries a checkbox, and saving it raises the restart dialog the box already shows for settings that only take effect at startup. Because the uplink is connected at startup, what is saved and what is running can differ for a minute, and the tab says which of the two it is in rather than going quiet: the pairing button stays disabled until the box reports the uplink actually running, so nobody presses it and gets nothing back.
