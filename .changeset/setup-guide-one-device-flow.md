The setup guide adds devices the way Settings does and tests each one when it is added. A device that does not answer stays out until you fix it or choose Add anyway, and the driver's setup hint says what to turn on. Chargers are added as devices. A new box starts with the planner on and without selling from the battery. The guide asks the price area once and chooses none for you; with no area it saves no prices and says so, and a box whose area is not set now says "Price area not set, using SE3" in its status. The guide ends with a line to the FTW app for iPhone.
