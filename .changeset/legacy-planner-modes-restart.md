A box left on one of the old planner modes, "Planner (self)" or "Planner (cheap)", now starts in Passive arbitrage after a restart and saves it. Passive arbitrage, like both old modes, never sells from the battery. Unlike "Planner (self)", it can charge the battery from the grid in cheap hours. With the planner turned off, the box starts in self-consumption instead. Unknown saved modes still fall back to the default.
