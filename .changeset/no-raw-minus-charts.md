The dashboard no longer prints a minus sign on grid and battery power. Charts label their axes with sizes and name what sits on each side of zero: above it what adds to the grid meter's reading (import, charge, load, the car), below it what takes away (export, discharge, solar). Grid and battery values say their direction in a word, such as "500 W export" or "1.4 kW discharge", in tooltips, the live strip, targets, driver cards, the charger card's plan table, the per-phase watts under the fuse bars and the plan's safety note. A battery that misses its target reads "short" or "over" along the target's direction. The Overview flow shows each device's size with "charging" or "discharging" under it, and a battery FTW only observes says so under its title. Home use keeps its sign for now.
