Keep home battery charging out of the room a slow charger's raise still needs. When a charger's driver declares its response time, as Easee Cloud declares 180 s, the battery now counts a car's offer as not yet in the meter for that time, as the car's fuse limit already did, instead of 20 s. A charger that takes two minutes to start no longer pushes a 25 A phase to 32.6 A. A charger that declares no response time keeps the 20 s, so the battery and a car on a local charger still charge together within a minute of the car's start.
