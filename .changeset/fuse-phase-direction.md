A car can now start and rise while the house exports. Fuse protection read each phase's current as a magnitude, so 20 A of solar export counted as 20 A of import and the car never started. A phase now takes its direction from the same phase's power in the meter reading, and new load on an exporting phase has more room, not less. When a meter reports no phase power, its current still counts as import.
