The car comes before home battery charging, also when the battery does not cover the car. The battery now charges only from the solar surplus the car leaves. Before, with battery cover off, FTW took the car out of the meter reading, so the battery charged from solar the car was using while the site imported up to 10 kW. Battery cover off still means the battery never discharges into the car. A planned grid purchase keeps its charge. In manual peak shaving, a battery charge that was running when the car started now comes off while the site imports, instead of holding under the peak limit.
