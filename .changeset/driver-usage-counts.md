The driver lists now say how many homes run exactly that version, from one and up. The labels "untested", "in testing" and "verified on hardware" are gone. A box counts a driver only if it delivered readings that day, and reports its version. The daily report now says the battery to the nearest kWh, and every box that shares statistics sends it, whether or not its owner has said yes in Settings. FTW Cloud still keeps no report and no address.
