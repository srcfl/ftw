The box's price answer to the app, and FTW Cloud's price tile, now carry the VAT rate the box taxes the total at, as `vatPermille` (250 is 25 %). The rate is the one in force after the default, so a box that never set `vat_percent` says 250. With it the iPhone app can show the spot price with VAT at the box's own rate instead of a guess.
