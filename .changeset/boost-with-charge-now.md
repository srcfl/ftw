Boost from home battery now works together with Charge now. Charge now sets how much the car draws; a boost lets the home battery cover that draw above the reserve you choose, for the time you choose. Before, the boost button was disabled while Charge now ran, and starting Charge now ended a running boost, so a quick top-up before leaving always came from the grid. Fuse, battery and charger limits apply as before.
