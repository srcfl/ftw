Home Assistant keeps long-term statistics for FTW's driver values: power, current, voltage, temperature and state of charge are measurements, and a driver's lifetime energy totals count up. FTW's own workings (each driver's Online sensor and metrics, Plan Stale, the per-phase values, the peak ceiling and the setpoint sensors) move to the device page's Diagnostic group, and the device page shows FTW's version. The older switch that lets the home battery feed the car with no time limit shows only while it is on. In the Home Assistant app, FTW now finds the Mosquitto broker within a minute when you add it after FTW, with no restart. Entity names and ids do not change.
