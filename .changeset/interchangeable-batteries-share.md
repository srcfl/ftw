"ftw": patch

When all batteries have the same power per kWh, they share the plan's total again. In a planner mode FTW followed each battery's own share of the plan. The planner pays the same for a kWh on either battery, so it often put a whole slot on one and the next on the other: one battery worked while the other stood at 0 W, their charge levels drifted apart, and on a large load only one battery's planned rate was used and the rest came from the grid. FTW now splits the live battery target across such batteries by the energy each can still give or take. The fuller battery discharges faster and the emptier one charges faster, so their charge levels draw closer while they work. Both cover a large load within their power limits: a battery at its limit stays there and the others take the rest. A battery that reports it cannot charge or discharge hands its share to the others. In an idle slot, or when FTW does not know a battery's charge level, the split is by capacity as before. Batteries whose charge or discharge limit per kWh differs by more than 5 %, such as one on a smaller hybrid inverter, still follow their own planned energy, and so does every battery in a fleet where any one differs. There is no new setting.
