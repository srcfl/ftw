The energy tax refund figure for battery storage now counts in proportion. Whatever leaves a battery, to the house or the grid, carries the grid's share of what the battery held, after the battery's efficiency over the year. The old order counted the house's use as grid energy first and the export as other energy first, so a battery with solar almost never got a figure. The home box's 2026 hours now give 42 kWh instead of 0.
