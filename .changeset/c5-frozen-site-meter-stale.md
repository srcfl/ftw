"ftw": patch
---

Treat a site meter that repeats one reading as stale. When FTW moves its own batteries and chargers by more than 500 W in sum, the devices' own readings follow, and the meter's total and phase values still stay the same for 30 s and 3 polls after that, FTW stops dispatch, returns devices to their own control and raises no EV, as for a silent meter. From the first such repeat, battery dispatch treats the reading as held, as it does for a reading with an old timestamp: it holds the battery instead of acting on the same error again. This also holds for a meter frozen since FTW started and for a car plugged in during the freeze. A quiet site is never marked stale, nor is a meter that sends a new reading only every 10 s and loses one, nor are moves that cancel out at the meter, and the first changed reading reopens dispatch. Before, such a meter counted as fresh because each poll brought a new timestamp, and in a rig run the battery swung from +5 kW to -5 kW on 40 s of repeated data. An EV order counts only once the charger reports the car drawing it, so at a site with no battery a frozen meter is found 30 s after that, not sooner.
